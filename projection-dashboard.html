Methodology: Leads/mo = monthly budget ÷ cost per lead. Campaign split follows the account's existing 50% / 22% / 28% spend allocation ($45 / $20 / $25 daily) unless you change the total budget, in which case each campaign scales proportionally. Service-level lead share uses the account's historical distribution (Public Adjuster 35%, Water Damage 25%, Roof Damage 15%, Hurricane/Storm 15%, Denied Claims 5%, Fire Damage 5%). This is a planning model, not a guarantee — real CPL moves with seasonality, competitor bidding, and landing page conversion rate, and hurricane season (Jun–Nov) typically shifts demand sharply toward storm-related ad groups.
