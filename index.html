01

Performance Overview

Executive Summary

Across the January–May 2026 reporting period, Foremost's paid search account matured steadily — a 40% reduction in cost per lead and a near-doubling of conversion rate, achieved on a flat budget. In parallel, the organic foundation was rebuilt: 19 technical issues resolved, 24 pages re-optimized, and a 140-keyword content map laid down. The account now sits on strong fundamentals with one urgent fix and three clear growth levers.

Main findings — what is working

  • CPL fell 40% from $190 (Jan) to $114 (Apr) with no budget increase — pure efficiency.
  • Conversion rate nearly doubled, 5.66% → 10.27%, signalling better ad-to-page alignment.
  • The DSA engine produced 36 of 48 tracked leads at $108.78 CPL — the lowest in the account.
  • Mobile is the business: 43 of 48 conversions (89.6%) at $92.83 CPL, well below blended.
  • SEO foundation rebuilt: 19/19 crawl issues fixed, 24 pages rewritten, 140 keywords mapped.

Main findings — what needs attention

  • May regression: CPL rose 25% ($114→$143) on identical spend — a post-click issue, not traffic.
  • Impression share 23% while competitors at <10% share appear top-of-page 65–86% of the time.
  • Desktop crisis: $397.95 CPL at 2.7% CVR — 4.3× mobile cost.
  • Budget waste: DSA triggering competitor-brand and Foremost-Insurance queries.
  • Organic invisibility: average position 25 — real impressions, almost no clicks yet.

Three forces shaping the South Florida claims market

  1. Seasonality is structural. Hurricane season (June–November) shifts demand sharply toward storm, roof, and water ad groups. Campaigns and landing pages must be pre-built to activate within 24 hours of a named storm.
  2. Geography is uneven. Miami-Dade is the volume core but the priciest market ($164 CPL); Broward and Orlando convert far more efficiently ($69 and $73) and are under-funded.
  3. Mobile is the medium. Nine in ten conversions happen on a phone. A claim decision starts as a thumb-typed "near me" search — the experience has to be built for that first.

1

Diagnose the May drop first

Compare May vs. April landing pages in GA4 and session recordings; A/B test restoring April's state. Highest-priority action before hurricane demand peaks.

2

Single-step mobile form

With mobile at 89.6% of conversions, any form over 3 fields leaks leads. Reduce to Name · Phone · Damage Type and add prominent click-to-call.

3

Sticky above-the-fold CTA

Add a persistent "Get Free Claim Review" button on the primary LP. Test urgency headlines ("Don't Accept a Low Settlement").

4

Fix the homepage routing

88 paid clicks landed on the homepage — a keyword-to-page mismatch. Configure DSA URL filters to route to service-specific pages.

5

Trust signals on every LP

Florida license number, BBB accreditation, testimonials, and the "No Recovery, No Fee" guarantee above the fold — claim decisions are high-consideration.

6

Core Web Vitals audit

Mobile speed currently unmeasured. A 1-second delay can cut conversions up to 20%. Target sub-3-second LCP on mobile.
