Landing Page Optimization Recommendations
1
Primary LP (/insurance-claims-help/): Highest traffic LP. Add a sticky "Get Free Claim Review" CTA button visible above the fold on mobile. A/B test the headline with urgency variants ("Don't Accept a Low Settlement").
2
Homepage redirect audit: 88 paid clicks landing on the homepage indicates keyword-to-LP mismatch. DSA campaigns may be routing to homepage instead of service-specific pages. Configure URL filters in DSA to exclude non-conversion pages.
3
Geo landing pages: The /miami/ and /coral-gables/ pages are already receiving paid traffic. Build dedicated pages for Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, and Palm Beach to support location-based campaigns.
4
Mobile form friction: Given mobile accounts for 89.6% of conversions, any form with more than 3 fields is losing leads. Implement click-to-call prominently, and use a single-step form (Name + Phone + Damage Type). Test Google Lead Form extensions.
5
Page speed critical: Mobile speed scores show "--" (not measured). A 1-second delay in mobile load time reduces conversions by up to 20%. Run Core Web Vitals audit via Google PageSpeed Insights and target sub-3-second LCP on mobile.
6
Trust signals above the fold: Add client testimonials, Florida license number, BBB accreditation, and "No Recovery, No Fee" guarantee prominently on all landing pages — insurance claim decisions are high-consideration.

Executive Conclusion

Foremost Public Adjusters has demonstrated consistent campaign maturation over the January–May 2026 reporting period, achieving a 40% reduction in Cost Per Lead and near-doubling of conversion rates without any corresponding increase in media investment. These efficiency gains validate the ongoing optimization work and confirm that the account's targeting and ad-to-landing-page alignment are improving quarter-over-quarter.

April 2026 established the performance benchmark the account should return to and surpass: 15 leads at $114 CPL with 10.27% CVR. The May pullback, while concerning, is diagnostic in nature — stable CPC, CTR, and spend isolate the issue to a post-click conversion experience change rather than any market or competitive shift. Addressing this before June 1 hurricane season is the campaign's single highest-priority action.

The account's critical growth leverage points are: (1) significantly underfunded impression share at 23% versus competitors operating at <10% but appearing at top of page 65–86% of the time; (2) the HTS All Services campaign generating leads at $48 CPL and requiring immediate scale; and (3) the mobile-first opportunity — a device segment driving 89.6% of conversions at $92.83 CPL that warrants dedicated bid strategies, form optimization, and lead form extensions. Foremost enters hurricane season with strong campaign fundamentals and a clear roadmap to sub-$120 CPL at scale.
