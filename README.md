## 🎯 Executive Summary

> **Engagement:** Full-funnel paid search audit, performance strategy, CRO analysis, SEO roadmap, and 90-day go-to-market plan for a South Florida public adjusting firm operating in one of the most competitive and time-sensitive insurance claim markets in the United States.

Foremost Public Adjusters helps South Florida homeowners and commercial property owners recover fair settlements from insurance companies following hurricane, water, roof, and fire damage events. As their Growth Marketing Specialist, I was engaged to audit a Google Ads account that had been running since January 2026, identify efficiency gaps, and build a scalable growth strategy ahead of the June 1 hurricane season demand window.

**The challenge was precise:** A consistent monthly media spend of ~$1,700 was generating between 7 and 15 leads per month with a Cost Per Lead ranging from $114 to $208 — and the team needed to understand *why* performance fluctuated and *how* to lock in the April 2026 peak efficiency (15 leads · $114 CPL · 10.27% CVR) as the new baseline.

**The finding was clear:** Every performance swing — the 40% CPL reduction from January to April, and the May reversal — was attributable to **conversion rate changes, not traffic quality or budget shifts.** Spend was virtually constant. CPC was stable. The opportunity was entirely post-click.

This case study documents the full diagnostic, the optimization strategy, and the 90-day execution roadmap built to scale Foremost PA's lead generation into and through hurricane season.

## 🏢 Business Context

### About the Client

**Foremost Public Adjusters** is a licensed public adjusting firm serving South Florida's residential and commercial property owners. Public adjusters are licensed professionals who represent policyholders — not insurance companies — in the claims process, negotiating to maximize settlement values on a contingency fee basis.

### Service Lines

| Service | Target Claim Type |
|---------|------------------|
| 🌀 Hurricane Damage Claims | Windstorm, storm surge, structural damage |
| 💧 Water Damage Claims | Burst pipes, flooding, plumbing failures |
| 🏠 Roof Damage Claims | Storm-related roofing, denied roof claims |
| 🔥 Fire & Smoke Damage | Residential and commercial fire losses |
| 📄 Insurance Claim Representation | Underpaid or denied claim management |
| 🦠 Mold Damage Claims | Secondary water intrusion events |

### Unique Value Proposition

```
No Recovery → No Fee
```

The contingency model eliminates financial risk for the property owner entirely. Foremost PA only earns a fee (typically 10–20% of settlement) when the client wins — making them the most accessible path to maximum claim recovery for any South Florida homeowner.

### Market Context

- South Florida has the **highest hurricane exposure** of any US metro
- Florida ranks #1 nationally for **insurance claim disputes per capita**
- Average public adjuster claim recovery: **20–40% higher** than insurer's initial offer
- Hurricane season (June–November) represents **60–80%** of annual claim volume

> **89.6% of all leads come from mobile phones.** This is not a secondary consideration — mobile is the primary conversion environment. A distressed South Florida homeowner discovering water damage in their kitchen at 10pm is reaching for their phone, not their laptop.

### Mobile Optimization Actions

1. **Apply +20% mobile bid modifier** — capture more high-intent mobile auctions
2. **Enable Google Lead Form Extensions** — leads captured directly in Google SERP, no LP visit required (estimated –15–25% CPL)
3. **Implement Call Ads** — for mobile users, a tap-to-call is often the highest-converting action
4. **Floating click-to-call button** on all mobile landing pages — visible without scrolling
5. **3-field form maximum** on mobile: Name + Phone + Damage Type (dropdown)

### Desktop Recovery Plan

1. **Immediate:** Apply –35% desktop bid modifier — stop overpaying for under-converting traffic
2. **Week 2–4:** Design desktop-specific LP variant with expanded trust section, FAQ accordion, client outcomes
3. **Month 2:** A/B test desktop LP variant vs. current page — target <$200 desktop CPL

## 🎯 Conclusion

This engagement demonstrates what separates strategic growth marketing from tactical ad management: the discipline to read data before making decisions, the judgment to know which lever to pull first, and the vision to build systems that compound in value over time.

**Three principles defined this engagement:**

**1. Efficiency Before Scale.**
The April 2026 breakthrough — 15 leads at $114 CPL from the same $1,710 spend that delivered 9 leads at $190 in January — was achieved entirely through conversion rate optimization, not budget increases. The most powerful paid search insight is often the simplest: before you scale, you must understand exactly why your best month was your best month.

**2. Device-First Strategy.**
89.6% of conversions came from mobile phones at $92.83 CPL. The 4.3× desktop CPL gap is not a technology problem — it is a strategic misalignment between traffic source and user experience. Solving it requires dedicated mobile UX investment, not higher bids.

**3. Seasonal Intelligence as Competitive Moat.**
For a South Florida public adjuster, hurricane season is not background noise — it is the primary growth event. The firms that build dedicated storm-response infrastructure, activate it within 24 hours of a named storm, and maintain surge budgets through the claim-filing window will generate the majority of their annual lead volume in a 5-month window. This case study provides that blueprint.

The 90-day roadmap outlined here is executable, data-driven, and built around one objective: giving Foremost Public Adjusters an unfair advantage in the market where they compete.
