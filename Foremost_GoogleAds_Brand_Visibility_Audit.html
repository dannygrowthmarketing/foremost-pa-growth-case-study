Executive Summary

Foremost Public Adjusters is not appearing on Google for searches on its own company name because the account's only brand-defense campaign ("CT AN Search Branded") is paused, and neither of the two Search campaigns currently running contains a single branded keyword. This is a structural account-management gap — not a competitive, technical, or algorithmic problem — and it is fully confirmed in the account's own keyword-level history.

The keyword-level data proves the client's recollection is accurate: the phrase-match keyword "foremost public adjusters" generated 46 impressions, 10 clicks, and $56.55 in spend before the campaign it lived in was switched off. Foremost did rank for its own name — it stopped only because the campaign stopped, not because a competitor outbid them, a negative keyword blocked them, or Google began favoring organic results.

We tested nine other possible explanations against the data provided and found no supporting evidence for any of them as a material cause. Two secondary issues did surface that deserve attention but are not the reason brand searches show no ad:

  • Foremost's non-brand ads currently trail two of three tracked competitors (flapublicadjusting.com, stellaradjusting.com) on Absolute Top-of-Page rate in generic auctions — a bid/rank issue on the core campaigns, separate from the brand gap.
  • Foremost's own historical broad-match keywords were shown bidding into competitors' brand searches (e.g., "stellar public adjusting," 1,806 impressions / $122.31 spent) — raising the real possibility competitors are doing the same in reverse. Unconfirmed pending a live check.
Recommended fix: rebuild a proper Brand Defense campaign — not the old 2-keyword version — within days. Estimated cost: $150–$300/month. This is the single highest-ROI fix available in the account today, and it is a same-week fix, not a rebuild project.

Root Cause Summary

Primary Root Cause — Confirmed

The account's only brand-defense campaign ("CT AN Search Branded") is paused, and the two Search campaigns currently running contain zero branded keywords between them. This is a structural account-management gap: when the account was restructured around the new "HTS-Jun10" architecture, the brand campaign was never carried forward or rebuilt. It is directly confirmed by keyword-level history showing the brand phrase-match keyword generated real impressions and clicks before the campaign was paused.

Contributing / Secondary Factors

  • The original brand campaign build was thin even when live — 2 keywords, $56.55 lifetime spend — so "previous" visibility was likely modest, not dominant, and worth improving on relaunch rather than simply restoring.
  • Category-wide brand-conquesting behavior is plausible: Foremost's own broad-match keywords were shown bidding into competitors' brand terms, suggesting the reverse is a realistic risk — unconfirmed pending a live SERP/Auction Insights check.
  • Foremost's non-brand ads currently under-index on Absolute Top-of-Page rate versus 2 of 3 tracked competitors — a related but separate bid/quality issue on the core generic campaigns.

Ruled Out — Not Supported by the Data

  • Negative keywords blocking the brand term specifically — no evidence found.
  • Performance Max or DSA absorbing branded clicks — no evidence at current spend/volume levels.
  • Google prioritizing organic over paid — mechanistically incorrect; there is no ad in the auction to be outranked.
  • Quality Score / Ad Rank collapse — indirect account-health signals are healthy; brand keywords typically score well by default.

Cannot Assess — Data Gaps to Close Before Finalizing

  • Campaign settings / location targeting (no Locations report provided)
  • Quality Score / Ad Rank at the keyword level
  • Live SERP check for "foremost public adjusters" (competitor presence, organic health)
  • Ad creative for "HTS-Jun10 – All Services #2"
  • Master negative keyword list at the account/shared-list level

Recommended Brand Campaign

Should a dedicated Brand Campaign be created?

Yes. The account should not simply reactivate the old campaign as-is — the original 2-keyword build was too thin to properly defend the brand. We recommend rebuilding it as a dedicated, always-on Brand Defense campaign with fuller keyword coverage, a defensive negative list, and a bid strategy designed to win the top slot on the company's own name at minimal cost.
